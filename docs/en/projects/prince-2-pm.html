P2PM, a structured approach to project management

P2PM is a project management application that turns a working method into a practical journey. Its current framework, inspired by PRINCE2 7, guides users from initial scoping to project review, connecting objectives, stakeholders, decisions, actions and documents.

From the brief to the review, in pictures

Nine screenshots take you through the overview, six stages, documents and PDF preparation. Account, client and project details have been redacted. Select a thumbnail or use the arrows to move through the journey; each image can be opened at full size.

What is the application for?

At each stage, P2PM helps users ask the right questions and keep the answers in one place. The project connects its objective, stakeholders, findings and the choices that follow. Users can move freely through the six stages, build up information gradually and prepare deliverables from work already entered. The current framework draws on PRINCE2 7 and adapts to the project context.

The user journey

  1. 01

    Define the assignment

    Give the project direction: expected outcome, scope, sponsor, resources and decision limits. The project brief, assumptions and budget help clarify open questions. Outcome: a scoping document that establishes a shared mandate and management rules.

  2. 02

    Connect stakeholders

    Understand who is involved and how information should flow. Organisation, person, interface and meeting records clarify roles, expected information and approval paths. Outcome: a stakeholder map showing who to involve and whom to contact when progress is blocked.

  3. 03

    Build the diagnosis

    Start from facts to understand difficulties and their effects. Findings connect situations to evidence; assumptions keep uncertainty visible; risks combine probability, impact and a planned response. Outcome: an evidence-based diagnosis and priorities to discuss with those involved.

  4. 04

    Design the solutions

    Compare options before committing. Scenarios examine benefits, cost, effort and feasibility; the RACI matrix clarifies responsibilities; deliverables and indicators define results to verify. Outcome: a target solution with practical criteria for assessing its success.

  5. 05

    Decide and plan

    Turn options into decisions and organised work. Decision, action, change, budget and exception registers connect choices, owners, deadlines and dependencies. Outcome: a decision briefing and delivery plan with traceable commitments.

  6. 06

    Deliver and learn

    Track delivery and verify the effects achieved. Actions, deliverables, indicators, risks and lessons learned help identify deviations, record acceptance and prepare handover. Outcome: a project review with lessons learned and follow-up work after closure.

What makes it useful

A framework for steady progress

Each stage asks a clear question and provides four checkpoints. Guidance and missing-information alerts help users build the project record without needing every answer at the outset.

Trace the reasoning behind a decision

A finding can link to supporting documents, the risk it reveals, a decision and the resulting actions. The team can trace the context behind a choice and the responsibilities attached to it.

The right view for the question

Stakeholder maps, a RACI matrix, scenario comparisons, a schedule, kanban and a risk matrix offer different views of the same records. Search and filters help users find priority issues.

Access aligned with responsibilities

Client workspaces distinguish administrators, users and readers. Read and write permissions are checked on the server. The MCP connector provides read-only access to authorized information.

A history of changes and recoverable data

The log records who made a change, when, and its before and after states. Archived records and documents remain recoverable; JSON and ZIP exports let users keep a copy of their work.

Documents that support the project

Documents support reasoning and decisions. P2PM brings sources together, shows what can be analysed and turns project information into packs that can be shared.

Collect documents and connect them

Upload multiple PDFs, Word or Excel files, text documents and images, then classify them and link them to the relevant records. Originals remain available, giving a finding or decision a direct link to its supporting documents.

Know which text is available

Extraction prepares usable text and indicates whether the result is complete, partial or unavailable. PDF text retains page references. This status helps identify documents that need to be read in their original form, particularly scans and images.

Choose sources and review AI analysis

Users prepare an archive of selected documents and context for their ChatGPT account, or use the read-only MCP connector. They can then review, correct and select proposals before importing them. The exports and proposal format could also support a local AI model, with an adapter still to be developed.

Compose the eight PDF packs

Executive summary, project brief, stakeholders and interfaces, diagnosis, target solution, decision briefing, progress report or complete pack: each format reuses information already entered. Users choose the registers and contact details to include, then download the PDF or save it in the project. The client administrator can customise the logo, fonts and layout.

A choice of methods in development

A choice of additional methods is in development: Scrum, PMI project management approaches, HERMES and other frameworks. The aim is to adapt the journey, questions and deliverables to the chosen method. The currently available journey remains the one inspired by PRINCE2 7.

Current scope

Application screenshots have been anonymized to present the features without exposing project data. The workflow adapts principles from PRINCE2 7; authorized people record approvals, which do not constitute electronic signatures. ChatGPT analysis and reviewed proposal imports are available. A direct connection to a local AI model remains to be developed. Extraction processes text already present in files; Excel formulas are not recalculated. Free-text report content should be reviewed before sharing.

My contribution

I design and develop P2PM with AI assistance. I translate project management needs into practical stages, linked records and documents that support decisions, while developing access rules and a record of changes.

What I built

  • A journey inspired by PRINCE2 7, organised into six stages and 24 checkpoints, with guidance to clarify missing information before making decisions.
  • Linked records and several working views: stakeholder maps, a RACI matrix, scenario comparisons, an action schedule and a risk matrix.
  • A document workspace with text extraction, links to records and eight configurable PDF packs, whose appearance can be customised by the client administrator.
  • An analysis workflow using ChatGPT with documents selected by the user, followed by proposal import after review. The exports and JSON format also provide a basis for using a local AI model, with an adapter still to be developed.
  • A React and TypeScript interface, SQL data and document storage, with server-side access checks, revision control and a read-only MCP connector.

What the project offers

Help the team understand its current position, what needs clarification and which decision will move the project forward. The information entered supports tracking and project packs, giving discussions a shared context.
