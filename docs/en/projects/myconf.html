MyConf, AI translation for conferences

A speaker presents in French while the audience listens on their phones to an English translation, or another language supported by ChatGPT. MyConf connects ChatGPT Voice to a shared listening stream through a QR code, with controls designed for live use.

What is the application for?

A speaker presents in French while the audience listens to an English translation, or another language supported by ChatGPT, using their phones and headphones. The organiser prepares the instructions in ChatGPT Voice, and MyConf shares the translated audio through a QR code. A session broadcasts one common language to all its listeners.

The user journey

  1. 01

    Prepare the context and language

    The organiser gives ChatGPT the conference topic, relevant terminology and target language. They can request a spoken translation that preserves meaning and smooths out hesitations instead of reproducing them word for word.

  2. 02

    Open MyConf and share the QR code

    Using Chrome on a computer, the organiser opens the extension controls, prepares a session and shares its QR code. This preparation does not start the broadcast.

  3. 03

    Speak and start broadcasting the translation

    The organiser starts ChatGPT Voice, then starts the MyConf broadcast once the connection is ready. ChatGPT receives the speech and produces the translation. MyConf relays its voice output to listeners with a short delay.

  4. 04

    Listen on a phone

    Each participant scans the QR code, opens the listening page and taps Listen. Listeners do not need a ChatGPT account or the extension. Headphones allow them to follow the translation without disturbing the room.

  5. 05

    Stay in control during the conference

    The organiser can mute their local audio while continuing the broadcast, pause and resume with the same QR code. A local caption window can supplement the presentation on the organiser’s screen.

  6. 06

    End the session

    End session invalidates listening access and starts clearing the audio buffer. The product is not intended to provide a complete recording of the conference.

What makes it useful

Make a conference accessible in another language

The speaker keeps their presentation language while the audience follows a spoken translation on their own phones. The QR code makes it easy for several listeners to join the same stream.

Prepare translation with context

The ChatGPT conversation provides a place to supply the topic, specialist terms and rephrasing instructions. The aim is to preserve the meaning of spoken language, including hesitations. Quality depends on the model, the instructions and the audio input.

Reuse ChatGPT Voice without an additional translation API

MyConf relays the voice produced by the existing session, without additional translation API charges. ChatGPT access and usage limits still apply, as do relay and hosting resources.

A simple experience for participants

A link or QR code, a phone and headphones are enough to join the stream. Listeners do not need to install the extension or have a ChatGPT account.

Controls designed for live use

Preparation, start, pause, resume and end are separate actions. The organiser can mute their local audio without interrupting the audience. Local captions remain an independent option.

Product development

An initial version of the Chrome extension has been submitted to the Chrome Web Store for review. A dedicated mobile application is in development.

Current scope

The Chrome prototype has been tested locally. Validation of complete conferences in real conditions is still ongoing. The language is set in the ChatGPT conversation, so all listeners in a session receive the same output, without individual selection of simultaneous languages. Translation may contain errors, and continuity depends on the browser and network.

My contribution

I design and develop, with AI assistance, the experience connecting the speaker and their listeners: the Chrome extension, session preparation, QR sharing, audio relay and broadcast controls. The aim is to make a presentation accessible in another language using the organiser’s ChatGPT Voice conversation.

What I built

  • An organiser workflow to open a session, display the QR code and start, pause, resume or end the broadcast. Local playback can be muted without cutting the audio sent to the audience.
  • Relay of ChatGPT’s translated voice to a listening page that works on a phone, without requiring a ChatGPT account for listeners. The organiser sets the language and contextual instructions in their conversation.
  • Continuous processing of the remote audio track by a bundled AudioWorklet, with two-second mono PCM blocks, WAV encoding and sequencing for the relay.
  • Explicit interruption and recovery handling, preserving the QR code during pauses and invalidating access when the session ends.
  • An optional floating window for local captions, with adaptive text sizing and light or dark themes. These captions remain separate from the audio stream sent to listeners.
  • Lifecycle tests, block continuity checks under load and PCM16 fidelity checks at 48 kHz.

What the project offers

Enable an audience to follow a conference in a common language chosen for the session, with simple access through a QR code. Context provided to ChatGPT can guide translation and the rephrasing of hesitant speech. MyConf reuses ChatGPT Voice without additional translation API charges. ChatGPT access, its usage limits and relay resources still apply.
