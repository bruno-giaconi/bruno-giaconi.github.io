AI Moves Fast.
Stay Curious, Stay Agile.

Stay curious. Keep the freedom to experiment.

In this talk, I share how I explore the capabilities and limits of AI models: testing, building and adapting my methods as the tools evolve.

With reno.com, my 2D and 3D interior planning app, I show how these experiments take shape in a working product. The guiding idea is to stay agile and retain the freedom to evolve your tools and platforms.

25 NOVEMBER 2026 · GENEVA

Who Gets
the Credit?

Ego, AI and creative authorship.

Mindstone Geneva AI Meetup
L’Annexe by Rentes Genevoises
Rue du Port 3, Geneva · 6 pm to 9 pm CET

View the 25 November programme ↗

On 25 November, I return to the Geneva AI Meetup with a more philosophical approach: when AI contributes to a creation, who deserves the credit? What remains ours: the result, the gesture, the intention, the vision?

I explore the role of ego, our need for recognition and creative authorship. From art to dance, one question keeps returning: when a person and a machine respond to one another, who can claim the gesture?

This reflection also considers debates about plagiarism and the detection of AI-generated content. It invites us to look differently at the value of our contribution, through the framing, choices and vision that guide creation.

“Virtuosity is not a matter of production, but rather of vision.”

Bruno Giaconi

A conviction behind this reflection: “AI pushes us to rise to a higher level; our ego will eventually accept it.”
